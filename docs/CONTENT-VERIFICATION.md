# Powertech Engineers — Content Verification Checklist

This audit checklist lists all critical company data points that **MUST BE FORMALLY VERIFIED** against the official Powertech Company Profile document or client authorization before moving to production publication.

> **CRITICAL DIRECTIVE**:
> Do not guess, invent, or resolve discrepancies autonomously. Every unverified item must remain in an unverified state until confirmed with supporting documentation.

---

## 1. Corporate Identity & Legal Registrations

| Item                                             | Current Status        | Source Reference Required    | Verification Notes / Discrepancies                                                          |
| :----------------------------------------------- | :-------------------- | :--------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Official Registered Name**                     | ⚠️ Needs Verification | Certificate of Incorporation | Confirm exact registered entity name (e.g. Pvt Ltd, LLP, Partnership, Sole Proprietorship). |
| **Incorporation Year**                           | ⚠️ Needs Verification | Statutory Filings            | Exact year company was established / incorporated.                                          |
| **Corporate Identification No. (CIN / Reg No.)** | ⚠️ Needs Verification | RoC / State Registrar        | Confirm official registration number.                                                       |
| **GSTIN (GST Number)**                           | ⚠️ Needs Verification | GST Certificate              | Valid 15-digit GSTIN.                                                                       |
| **Permanent Account Number (PAN)**               | ⚠️ Needs Verification | Income Tax Department        | Company PAN card copy.                                                                      |
| **Official Corporate Tagline**                   | ⚠️ Needs Verification | Company Profile PDF          | Confirm approved corporate motto / brand slogan.                                            |
